Privacy Policy — Simple Music Player

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Simple Music Player is a free, straightforward music player app for Android devices. It is 100% free of ads and subscriptions, and works offline.

1. Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, transmit, store on servers or share personal data. It does not use user accounts, analytics tools or an internet connection.

2. Local storage

The app stores only on the device:

  • Playlists and favorite songs;
  • Play queue and position of the last song played;
  • Repeat and shuffle mode preferences.

This data is not sent to third parties nor included in Android's automatic backup. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes this information.

3. Permissions

  • Music and audio: lets the app find and play audio files stored on the device. Files are not sent to third parties. They are only renamed or moved to the device trash when the user chooses that option in the app and confirms it in Android's own dialog.
  • Background playback: keeps music playing with the screen off and provides controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
